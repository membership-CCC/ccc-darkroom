CCC DARKROOM — HOW TO UPLOAD
════════════════════════════════════════════════

Make a folder in here, put the photos in it, walk away.
Processed within ~15 minutes plus sync time.


1. NAME THE FOLDER
────────────────────────────────────────────────

   2026-08-01_borderlands

   date_label   — the date carries into every filename.

   Letters, numbers, hyphens, underscores ONLY.
   No slashes. No spaces.

   Drive lets you type a slash. macOS does not allow
   one in a folder name, so the folder silently never
   reaches the machine that does the work. This has
   already cost us one roll.


2. ADD THE PHOTO CREDIT
────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Put _by_ and the handle on the end:

   2026-08-01_borderlands_by_donalrey
   2026-08-01_borderlands_by_the_catskill_weekender

   Every export from that folder gets a small
   @handle bottom-left. Leave _by_ off and nothing
   is marked.

   Underscores inside a handle are fine.
   Don't include the @ — it's added for you.


3. TWO PEOPLE SHOT THE SAME RIDE?
────────────────────────────────────────────────

   One folder each, SAME label, different _by_:

   2026-08-01_borderlands_by_donalrey
   2026-08-01_borderlands_by_the_catskill_weekender

   Both land in one roll — 2026-08-01_borderlands —
   numbered straight through, each frame carrying
   its own credit, one contact sheet for the ride.

   The label must match exactly or you get two
   separate rolls.

   A third person can add their folder days later.
   The roll grows and the contact sheet rebuilds.


4. UPLOAD INTO THE FOLDER
────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Not into _dump itself. Loose files here are
   ignored on purpose — no folder means no label
   and no date.

   Big uploads: the folder appears in Drive long
   before the photos actually arrive on the machine.
   Give a few hundred MB several minutes before
   expecting anything.


WHAT COMES BACK
────────────────────────────────────────────────

   CCC/Photos/2026-08-01_borderlands/
       instagram/  strava/  email/  web/
       contact_sheet.png   <- cull from this
       manifest.csv
       decisions.txt       <- fill this in
       _hold/              <- see below

   Note the output folder name is lowercased and
   hyphenated. Borderlands_Don becomes
   borderlands-don. If you can't find a roll, look
   for the lowercase form before assuming it failed.


WHOSE PHOTO IS WHOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Your originals are kept, filed by photographer:

   CCC/Photos/_originals/2026-08-01_borderlands/
       by_donalrey/
       by_the_catskill_weekender/

   Same filenames as uploaded, untouched, never
   marked. Two people can use the same filename
   without either being lost.


_hold/ — READ THIS ONE
────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Anything showing a road sign, junction name or
   trailhead marker gets held out of the publish
   folders. HOLD_REVIEW.txt says why.

   We publish photos of our own members riding
   known routes on a predictable schedule. Enough
   readable signage tells a stranger where we'll
   be and when.

   Look at held frames yourself before using them.


NOTHING IS PUBLISHED AUTOMATICALLY
────────────────────────────────────────────────

   Every row comes out marked "draft". Posting and
   sending stay manual, on purpose.


────────────────────────────────────────────────
Good:  2026-08-01_borderlands
       2026-08-01_borderlands_by_donalrey
       2026-07-31_shakedown-ride

Bad:   _dump/2026-08-01_borderlands   (slash)
       Borderlands 8/1                (slash, spaces)
       borderlands                    (no date)
